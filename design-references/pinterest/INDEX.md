# Pinterest references

Static design inspirations (no code). Use them for layout, palette, typography and component styling.

## web-app/

- `user-profile-kodecolor.webp` — clean light profile page on a saturated blue backdrop. Two columns: photo + work/skills list on the left, name, star rating, action row, tabs and a label/value contact grid on the right. Tiny uppercase section labels, lots of whitespace, a single blue accent. ([source](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:800/1*Sdfm4E98ugZGQ-u5Xoa83g.png))
- `hr-dashboard-talentasync.webp` — HR dashboard. Deep green header band flows into a cream body. Pill nav, "Good Morning" greeting, 4 KPI cards with delta chips plus an "Add new widget" card, list cards (jobs, interviews), a segmented bar for employment status and a dot-matrix attendance heatmap. ([source](https://i.pinimg.com/originals/de/e0/fd/dee0fd83ee6c224468ee15ffb8086c0a.jpg))
- `user-profile-matdash.webp` — admin template profile. Icon rail plus a labeled sidebar, soft lavender palette, a 3D-illustration cover banner with an overlapping avatar, stats and social buttons, a tab strip, an intro card and a post composer. ([source](https://i.pinimg.com/1200x/94/07/40/94074007e6b2d2b0e32edd09564ad277.jpg))
- `notes-dashboard-bento.webp` — bento-grid productivity dashboard. Large greeting headline, illustrated feature tiles, notifications with a lifted "card pulled out" swipe state, a calendar agenda, task progress rows, a violet "Go premium" card and ring-progress stats. Purple accent on off-white, with a curved sidebar shape. ([source](https://i.pinimg.com/1200x/d9/30/bc/d930bcb8736ed8b9c44cf49073e250c9.jpg))
- `cardiology-timeline-dashboard.webp` — medical treatment timeline. Warm grey UI inside a dark rounded device frame on neon yellow. Pill tabs, a patient card, vitals row, a branching horizontal timeline linking medication pills to chart cards, and a bottom month scrubber with badges. ([source](https://i.pinimg.com/originals/b2/bd/ea/b2bdea17b099b6ac5263238382b6b154.jpg))
